01 · BEFORE THE BIODATA

Perhaps first,

I am Osama — kind, caring, honest and somewhat introverted. I usually speak less when I first meet someone, but once I become comfortable, I can talk a lot.

I am shy and sensitive, and I can get hurt easily, but I do not stay upset for long. I am a good listener and I try to understand what people are feeling rather than judging them quickly.

I have a playful side too. I enjoy travelling, photography, anime, gaming, exploring new places and trying different kinds of food. I like natural places especially — mountains, waterfalls, rivers, green landscapes and rainy weather.
